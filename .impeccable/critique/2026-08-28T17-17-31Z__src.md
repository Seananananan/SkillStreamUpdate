## Design specificity

The color contract is authored for this product: teal means act, green means achieved, amber means not ready, red means failed. The certificate object plus public `/verify` is Skillstream. Almost everything around that climax is category-interchangeable: Geist on a slate canvas, Lucide + letter tiles, welcome-back dashboards, three stat cells, three quick-action rows. Landing sells a connected journey the way every integration pitch does; student Home then displays the architecture instead of the next lesson.

## Cognitive load

High on student Home and the lesson player (7 of 8 checklist failures: single focus, chunking, hierarchy, one-thing-at-a-time, minimal choices, working memory, progressive disclosure). Grouping via cards passed. Login, catalog, and `/verify` stay low.

## Emotional journey

Peak: last-lesson complete → certificate page → public verify. Valley: welcome-back with zero enrollments, instructor-prefilled login, prototype video/quiz, Automated handoffs on student Home, empty Escalations after the landing promised help.

## What's working

1. The credential is a real object (record, reference, public verify).
2. Functional palette and Operate primitives (44px, labeled nav, skip link, focus-visible, reduced motion).
3. Empty states that name the next click.

## Priority issues

**[P1] Student Home is a brochure, not a next action**
Why: First authenticated screen stacks journey, stats, disabled actions, a dead open-enrollment list, and LMS handoffs.
Fix: If enrolled, one Continue block. If not, one catalog Enroll. Move IntegrationFlow off the student path.

**[P1] The lesson player is trapped in AppShell**
Why: On a phone the order is brand + 4-tab nav + title + full outline, then the lesson. Complete sits below the fold.
Fix: Lesson route: one exit, outline from lg up, Complete in the thumb zone. Do not mount four-item product nav on the player.

**[P1] First-run and landing sell a different product than the app**
Why: Sign in with no create-account; login defaults to instructor; landing nav hidden below md; copy says Download certificates.
Fix: Student-default login. Mobile: Verify + How it works. Copy: share a reference, don't download a file.

**[P2] Guardrails don't match the stakes**
Why: Empty-course publish is warned then allowed. Quiz option A is pre-selected. Success green is used for in-progress enrollment.
Fix: Disable Publish until there is content. Don't pre-check quiz answers. Reserve success for completed/certified/published.

**[P2] Help is advertised and then absent**
Why: Landing promises help as you go; Escalations is always empty.
Fix: Bury Planned automations on student Home. Honest "Need help?" in the player or remove the inbox from primary nav until it has records.

## Persona red flags

**Jordan:** Instructor-prefilled login; Welcome back at zero enrollments; disabled Continue/Certificates next to LMS handoffs; open-enrollment rows are not links.

**Casey:** Landing section links hidden below md; four squeezed student tabs; player outline before content; quiz radios under 44px.

**Sam:** Field focus removes outline; disabled quick actions are non-focusable divs; copy-reference failure is silent; skip-link z-index vs sticky header.

## Minor observations

Page titles use clamp on Operate surfaces. Enroll-Learn-Certify is drawn four times before the first task. Desktop logout is under 44px. Escalations sits in primary nav at a permanent 0.

## Questions to consider

- If a returning student may do only one thing, why is Home anything other than Continue {next lesson}?
- If certification is the peak, why is the first logged-in screen about LMS handoffs?
- What would the player look like if AppShell were chrome you leave, not a wrapper the lesson lives inside?
